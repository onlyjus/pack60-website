# Maintenance Checklist

Review the public site monthly or seasonally.

## Checklist

- Check the Join page for clarity, current meeting guidance, and any facts waiting on human approval.
- Confirm the calendar embed still works.
- Review generic leader contact aliases.
- Check photos for public safety.
- Remove or update outdated dates.
- Check for broken links.
- Review the privacy and public content policy.
- Review the private member calendar for outdated events.
- Review the current and prior month's budget rows for accuracy.
- Rotate or revoke calendar subscription links reported as lost or shared.
- Review member photo albums for removal requests and approved access only.
- Review the Cloudflare Access application, login methods, and session policy.
- Review active and removed accounts in the member admin dashboard.
- Revoke expired or abandoned invitation links.
- Confirm at least two trusted adults retain administrator access.
- Review member access audit records and D1 recovery readiness.
- Archive old content that is no longer useful.
- Update recruiting information before joining season.
- Confirm no private youth, parent, roster, address, meeting link, payment, or advancement data has been added.
