## Pack calendar

This calendar page highlights Pack 60's regular meeting rhythm and common event types.

Pack 60 meets Tuesdays at 7:00 PM at [Suncrest United Methodist Church, 479 Van Voorhis Road](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=479+Van+Voorhis+Rd%2C+Morgantown%2C+WV), in Morgantown, WV. Registered families should still confirm schedule changes through official pack communications.

For current public announcements and recent activity highlights, visit the [Pack 60 Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/pack60wv/).

Registered families can use the [member calendar](/members/calendar/) for private event details after access is approved.

Mountaineer Area Council also maintains a [Council Calendar](https://mountaineerscouting.org/calendar/) for district and council events.

## Common event types

- Pack meeting
- Tuesday meeting at Suncrest United Methodist Church
- Join Scouting night
- Fall family campout
- Spring family campout
- Pinewood Derby
- Blue & Gold
- Service project
- Veterans Parade or other community event
- Hiking or outdoor skills activity
- Rain gutter regatta
- STEM or science activity
- Summer activity
