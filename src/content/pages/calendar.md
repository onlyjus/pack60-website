## Our annual rhythm

There is something to look forward to in every season. This year-at-a-glance highlights the larger events Pack 60 commonly plans; exact dates, availability, and final details are confirmed with families each year.

Pack 60 also maintains a regular Tuesday meeting rhythm at [Suncrest United Methodist Church, 479 Van Voorhis Road](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=479+Van+Voorhis+Rd%2C+Morgantown%2C+WV), in Morgantown.

## Winter adventures

### January

- WVU Planetarium pack meeting
- Sledding or tubing at Chestnut Ridge Park, weather permitting

### February

- Pinewood Derby build night
- Arrow of Light crossover
- Pack 60 Pinewood Derby

### March

- WVU STEM Day and Council Pinewood Derby
- Bowling pack meeting

## Spring outdoors and celebration

### April

- Scouting for Food community service
- West Virginia Botanic Garden hike
- Spring family campout

### May

- Blue & Gold Banquet
- Optional family trip to Kennywood

## Summer adventures

### June

- Cub Scout Day Camp
- Rail-trail cleanup service project
- Picnic and fishing at Chestnut Ridge Park

### July

- Morgantown Fourth of July Parade
- West Virginia Black Bears Scout Night
- Cub Scout resident camp
- Tygart Lake Adventure Park outing

### August

- Ice skating at Mylan Park
- Welcome Back pack meeting
- Family pool party

## Fall camping and community

### September

- Fall recruiting and family fun event
- Fall family campout at Camp Mountaineer
- Raingutter Regatta

### October

- Fall Cub Adventure Weekend
- Fall leaf hike
- Trunk or Treat at Camp Mountaineer
- Halloween pack meeting

### November

- Cubsgiving with another local pack
- Morgantown Veterans Day Parade
- Outdoor cold-weather skills event

### December

- Camp Mountaineer winter hike
- Holiday pack meeting with service and treats
