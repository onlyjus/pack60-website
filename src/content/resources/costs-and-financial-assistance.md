Cub Scout costs can include national registration, local pack dues, uniform items, handbook materials, and optional activity expenses.

A recent Pack 60 welcome flyer listed an annual national registration fee and a local pack fee. The local pack fee helps cover shared pack expenses such as awards, Pinewood Derby cars, and other pack program materials.

Fees can change by year and family situation, so families should confirm current costs with Pack 60 before registering or making purchases.

## Uniform costs

The scout uniform is a separate cost from registration and pack dues. Pack 60 welcome materials describe the uniform shirt as the minimum uniform item. Families should review [uniform basics](/resources/uniform-basics/) and ask pack leadership before buying optional uniform pieces.

## Assistance

Pack 60 believes every child should have the opportunity to participate in Scouting. If cost is a concern, contact [membership@pack60.org](mailto:membership@pack60.org) and ask about available assistance through council or pack channels.

## Fundraising

Pack fundraisers help support shared adventures and keep activity costs manageable. Fundraising details, dates, and family instructions are shared through official pack communications.
