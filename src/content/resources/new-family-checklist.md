- Read the Join page.
- Contact the pack at [cubmaster.pack60wv@gmail.com](mailto:cubmaster.pack60wv@gmail.com?subject=Pack%2060%20membership%20question).
- Ask about visiting a Tuesday 7:00 PM meeting at [Suncrest United Methodist Church](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Suncrest+United+Methodist+Church%2C+Morgantown%2C+WV).
- Use official registration links from pack leadership.
- Review the [costs and financial assistance](/resources/costs-and-financial-assistance/) resource before registration.
- Review [uniform basics](/resources/uniform-basics/) and ask a leader about rank-specific items before buying.
- Review the [annual program rhythm](/resources/annual-program-rhythm/) to see common Pack 60 activities.
- Complete the Pack 60 welcome forms, including the Pack-specific media-release choice and annual fundraising commitment.
- Provide a frequently checked email address and cell phone number for each participating adult.
- Join Pack 60's GroupMe using the current invitation provided directly by pack leadership. GroupMe is the pack's primary channel for general announcements.
- Each year, complete Scouting America's [How to Protect Your Children from Child Abuse: A Parent's Guide](https://www.scouting.org/training/youth-protection/parents-guides/) exercises with your Scout for the Bobcat Adventure requirement.
