Pack 60 has an active school-year program with den meetings, pack meetings, outings, service, and seasonal traditions. Exact dates, arrival details, and sign-up instructions are shared through official pack communications.

## School-year rhythm

- Weekly den or pack meetings on many Tuesdays during the school year
- About one pack meeting per month where all dens gather together
- Fall and spring family campouts
- Community events such as parades or public Scouting activities
- Service projects such as weather-readiness or food-collection efforts
- Pinewood Derby
- Blue & Gold celebration
- Outdoor activities such as hikes, nature programs, and skills practice
- STEM or science activities

## Summer rhythm

Pack 60 does not usually hold the same regular den meeting schedule in the summer. Summer activities may include camps, hikes, picnics, baseball nights, water activities, service meetups, or other optional family-friendly events when leaders and families are available.

## How families get details

Public pages describe the general rhythm. Registered families receive current details, schedule changes, permission requirements, costs, and arrival instructions through official pack communication channels.
