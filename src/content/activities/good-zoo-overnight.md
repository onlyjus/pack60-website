## What it was

Pack 60 families took part in a special overnight experience at the Good Zoo in Wheeling, WV. The adventure included exploring animal exhibits, gathering around a campfire, and rolling out sleeping bags beside an exhibit for the night.

## Why it was memorable

The zoo overnight combined wildlife learning with the excitement of spending the night somewhere unusual. Scouts had time to observe animals, ask questions, share the experience with friends, and make memories beyond a regular meeting.

## What families should know

Special overnights depend on venue availability, pack planning, and current program requirements. Past activities are examples of the kinds of adventures Pack 60 families may experience and do not guarantee that the same event will be offered every year.
