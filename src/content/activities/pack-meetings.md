## What it is

Pack meetings bring all dens together for activities, ceremonies, announcements, recognition, advancement celebrations, and family participation.

## Recent pack activities

In an [October 28, 2026 Facebook post](https://www.facebook.com/pack60wv/posts/pfbid02SQ1iLmo4f5Edzw69jsZUFdZEou8EJA9Bt3XHVW6J2dJAGbGrj2ogT5Hma49y8w67l), Pack 60 shared Halloween fun with costumes and group activities. The two Halloween photos above come from that public post.

An earlier [September 23, 2026 Facebook recap](https://www.facebook.com/pack60wv/posts/pfbid023yxmWgxgUoeStpJVqi74LPSvh7TKGviTgTu1aNKYWZCgUx1Bf6PzEVAoukaBNxaCl) featured scouts racing boats made from recycled materials at the Raingutter Regatta.

## When it usually happens

Pack 60 meets Tuesdays at 7:00 PM at [Suncrest United Methodist Church](https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=Suncrest+United+Methodist+Church%2C+Morgantown%2C+WV) in Morgantown, WV. About once a month, the Tuesday meeting is a pack meeting with all dens together. Schedule changes and special event details are shared through official pack communication channels.

## Why scouts enjoy it

Scouts see friends from other dens, participate in larger activities, and celebrate progress.

## What families should know

Parents and guardians should expect to stay, participate, and help their scouts. Families visiting for the first time should contact the pack before attending so a volunteer can confirm arrival details.
